Privacy Policy - Build My Block House

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Build My Block House (package name: com.wisimme.buildmyblockhouse) is a creative building game published by Wisimme.

1. Game Information

The game does not require account registration or ask players to enter their name, email address, or other personal details to play.

2. Game Progress

Houses, decorations, and progress are stored locally on the device. The game does not provide cloud synchronization or user accounts.

3. Advertising and Data

The game uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements in the house editor and optional rewarded advertisements. Special decorations start locked. A decoration is permanently unlocked only when the advertising SDK confirms that its reward was earned. Houses can be built and saved without watching these optional videos. Unlock records are stored locally. The current validation build uses Google's official test rewarded ads; production rewarded advertising has not been enabled pending verification of Google Play Families requirements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process technical data, including IP-derived approximate location, device information, identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic data for advertising delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

Advertising requests are configured for child-directed treatment, with a maximum ad content rating of G and non-personalized ads. The app does not request the Android Advertising ID permission.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children's Privacy

Build My Block House is intended for a family audience, including children. Players are not required to submit personal information. Child-directed advertising settings are applied to all users.

5. Data Security

Game progress is stored locally on the device. Data transmitted by the advertising SDK is encrypted in transit.

6. Third-Party Services

The game uses Google AdMob for advertising. Google's data practices are described in its privacy policy linked above.

7. Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be updated when the game, advertising services, or applicable requirements change.

8. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact: wisimme@gmail.com
